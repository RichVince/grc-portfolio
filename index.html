Meridian Consulting Group is a 22-employee professional services company operating in a Microsoft 365 cloud environment. The organization has limited internal IT resources, no dedicated security team, partial MFA deployment, limited logging visibility, and no formal risk register.

Program Maturity

Before and after the GRC assessment.

This shows the business value of the audit work: moving from informal security practices toward documented, framework-aligned governance and control improvement.

Before Assessment

Initial Control Gaps

  • No formal risk register or risk ownership process
  • Incomplete MFA deployment and weak access governance
  • Limited logging and no formal log review process
  • No documented backup testing or data classification approach
  • No structured vulnerability and patch management process
  • No formal vendor or security policy review process
After Recommendations

Improvement Roadmap

  • Access review and offboarding recommendations documented
  • MFA enforcement and alerting improvements prioritized
  • Logging, monitoring, and retention gaps identified
  • Backup testing and data protection improvements proposed
  • Patch management and asset inventory actions documented
  • Governance policy and vendor management improvements defined
